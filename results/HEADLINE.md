# Headline results

Across 6 paired seeds at matched missing rates (iid 0.249, grouped 0.250, informative 0.232), the configuration a standard iid benchmark would choose degrades by +0.44 days RMSE under grouped dropout and +0.49 days under informative dropout (+0.08 vs complete data). The illusion concentrates in the naive fills: under informative dropout the mean-fill cells degrade by +1.93 days on average and the worst cell (mean/single_sensor) by +6.41 days, while the same fills barely move under grouped dropout. The best imputer under iid is not the best under informative dropout in 37.5% of (seed, predictor) pairs and is outright the worst in 0.0%. The profiler names the planted regime with accuracy 1.000 (MAR screen 0.667, always-iid 0.333) and identifies the dropout driver with accuracy 1.000.
